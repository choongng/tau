## 1. Worker wall versus decode speed (cost: ~74 min of workers plus ~20 min of driver recovery)

- At 19–26 tok/s, the 900 s wall (DEFAULT_DISPATCH_TIMEOUT) is about 18–23k output tokens. The catalog gives
  max_tokens = 16384 for this model, so **one maximal turn is 650–850 s, nearly the whole wall**.
  65e647ef shows this: one turn of 818 s that hit the length limit. A 900 s wall makes sense for qwen3.6-35b at
  ~59 t/s, but it is not commensurate with q38f. The cap should scale with measured tok/s, or be
  expressed in tokens or turns, not seconds.
- Why the turns were huge: the thinking in workers derived expected values by hand. The paper gives rules,
  not worked numbers. The workers also hunted byte-exact `source` quotes through the PDF-text mojibake
  (█▒ glyphs, "Jjoin(e1 , e2 )K"). The driver's notes said "Read both fully", which contradicts the
  brief's write-early advice.
- A killed worker's turn in flight is lost: transcripts end at the last completed row (a4f69dee is ~7 min short).
  The kill result says "Split the task, or lower its turn cap". Neither fits here: the problem is
  per-turn length, not turn count.
- The positive signal is that the driver read the killed worker's partial reasoning and put it into the notes. The next worker
  (e15ea26e) then finished in 4 min. tau could do this salvage automatically by appending the killed worker's last
  thinking or text, and its uncommitted diff, to the retry's notes.
- **mining.md:** partly known. `stop:length/worker` (#7) and `worker_outcome:length/length_stop` (#16) are
  "not_a_bug" or "deferred", and `worker_outcome:timeout/*` (#21, #38) is not_a_bug or deferred. **New:** the
  wall is not commensurate with max_tokens at slow decode rates, and kill-then-retry has no salvage.

## 3. T0011's witness was vacuous (cost: low in time, high in trust)

- 25ab38cb's worker witnessed with tests/cases.json (a data witness). tau's `_is_test_path` (beagle.py:9113)
  treats **every non-.py file under tests/** as a test, so it refused that witness. The fallback then took away the
  files named in done_when, together with notes/paper_windows.md: the check's own script,
  tests/check_known_answers.py, was removed. The check failed only because its script was missing (file not found).
- beagle accepted the witness: `_own_new_tests` (pursuit.py:6553) refuses only when **every** site is a
  new test the check names ("a single site in the code under test makes the whole witness about that code").
  paper_windows.md was the non-test site, so the witness passed.
- At 14:47:04 the same take-away, done on its own for T0008, was **NOT PROVEN**. That confirms the T0011
  proof rested on the script vanishing.
- Verdict: **bugs on both sides, plus driver misuse.**
  - tau: the fallback must never take away files named by done_when. A JSON or data file under tests/ should be
    allowed as a witness for a known-answers task.
  - beagle: a witness should be refused when the check's failure comes from a site it names. A mixed site
    should not launder it.
  - Driver misuse: the driver bound a check (check_known_answers.py) that the worker itself wrote in the same step.
- **mining.md:** **new**. The nearest prior notes are R02:136 ("source file in the task's scope that could be
  taken away") and R31's mixed-scope fixes, which are about scope rather than witness sites.
